# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. As contributors and maintainers of the skool.lol project, we pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful**: Treat all community members with kindness and respect
- **Being inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds and experiences
- **Being constructive**: Provide feedback that is helpful and focused on improvement
- **Being collaborative**: Work together toward our shared goal of making school better
- **Being honest**: Communicate openly and truthfully in all interactions

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks or insults
- Hate speech or derogatory comments
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate content
- Spam or promotional content unrelated to the project
- Attempting to compromise or manipulate the platform
- Violating anyone's privacy or security

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. **Do not engage** with the offending behavior
2. **Report it** by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers
3. **Provide details** about what happened and when

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. We are committed to maintaining a safe and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, pull requests)
- Community interactions and events
- Official project communications

## Our Commitment

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

We reserve the right to remove contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct and to ban users who persistently violate our community standards.

## Thank You

By participating in skool.lol, you help create a better community for students everywhere. We appreciate your commitment to making our space safe, inclusive, and welcoming. 💖
